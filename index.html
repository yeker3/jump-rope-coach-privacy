English

Jump Rope Coach Pro collects no personal data. We, the developers, receive nothing from the app.

  • The app runs entirely on your watch. It has no internet access, no account, no analytics and no advertising.
  • It reads your watch's motion sensor to count jumps and, if available, your heart rate to show it on screen. These readings are processed on the watch and are not sent to us.
  • Your settings and personal records are stored only on your watch. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • When you save a session, it is stored as an activity in your own Garmin Connect account, like any other activity recorded on your watch. That data is handled by Garmin under Garmin's privacy policy; we have no access to it.
  • Purchases are processed by the Connect IQ Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Heart rate is shown for information only. This app is not a medical device.

Questions: keepfitapps@gmail.com

Deutsch

Jump Rope Coach Pro erhebt keine personenbezogenen Daten. Wir als Entwickler erhalten nichts von der App.

  • Die App läuft vollständig auf deiner Uhr. Sie hat keinen Internetzugang, kein Konto, keine Analyse und keine Werbung.
