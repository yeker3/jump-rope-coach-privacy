We collect no personal data

Sun Session is a watch app for Garmin Connect IQ. It does not collect, store on our side, share or sell any personal data. It has no account, no analytics and no network connection of its own.

  • Your skin type, SPF, place and settings are stored only on your watch.
  • The UV index and temperature come from the weather your watch already syncs with Garmin Connect. The app does not ask for your location.
  • Altitude, if your watch provides it, is read on the watch and never leaves it.
  • Your weekly time in the sun is stored only on your watch.

Sun Session is a reminder tool for good sun habits. It is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.
